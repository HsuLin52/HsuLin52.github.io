Easy Questions

2. What is the integral of 0 with respect to x?

Medium Questions

7. Evaluate the integral of 4x³ dx.

9. Which of the following functions is not differentiable at x = 0?

Hard Questions
11. Find the second derivative of f(x) = x⁴ - 3x³ + 2x² - x + 7.



12. Evaluate the definite integral of x² from 1 to 3.



13. If f(x) = sin(x), what is its second derivative?



14. If the derivative of a function is always positive, what can be said about the function?



15. Find the derivative of ln(x² + 1).
