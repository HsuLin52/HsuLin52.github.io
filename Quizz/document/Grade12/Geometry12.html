Geometry Grade 12 Quiz
Easy Questions
• Find the magnitude of the vector v = (3, 4).



• Which of the following is the equation of a circle centered at (0, 0) with radius 5?



• Find the slope of the line passing through points (2, 3) and (4, 7).



• Find the dot product of vectors a = (1, 2) and b = (3, 4).



• Which equation represents a parabola with vertex at (0, 0) opening upwards?



Medium Questions
• Which conic section is represented by the equation x² - y² = 16?



• Find the distance between points (1, 2) and (4, 6).



• Find the equation of a line passing through (3, -2) with slope 2.



• If a vector is perpendicular to (2, 3), which of the following could be its direction?



• Which conic section is represented by the equation 4x² - 9y² = 36?



• Find the parametric equations for a line passing through (1, 2) with direction vector (3, -1).



Hard Questions
• Find the angle between vectors a = (1, 2, 3) and b = (4, 5, 6).



• Find the equation of a plane passing through (1, -2, 3) with normal vector (2, 1, -4).



• Find the eccentricity of the hyperbola given by the equation x²/16 - y²/9 = 1.



• Find the equation of the tangent line to the ellipse x²/9 + y²/4 = 1 at point (3, 0).
