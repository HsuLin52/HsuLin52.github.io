Algebra Grade 11 Quiz
Easy Questions
1. What is the general form of a quadratic equation?



2. Which of the following is the determinant of a 2×2 matrix A = |2 3| |4 5|?



3. What is the square root of -1 in terms of complex numbers?



4. Which of the following represents a complex number?



5. What is the sum of the roots of the quadratic equation x² - 7x + 10 = 0?



Medium Questions
6. If A = |1 2| |3 4|, what is its determinant?



7. What is the product of the roots of x² - 5x + 6 = 0?



8. What is the conjugate of the complex number 4 + 3i?



9. If x² + px + 8 = 0 has roots -2 and -4, what is the value of p?



10. Solve for x in the equation x² + 4 = 0.



Hard Questions
11. Find the inverse of matrix A = |2 1| |5 3|.



12. What is the quadratic equation whose roots are 3 + i and 3 - i?



13. If A = |2 3| |1 4|, find A².



14. If a complex number z satisfies z² + z + 1 = 0, what are its roots?



15. Find the determinant of matrix A = |3 2 1| |4 1 5| |2 6 4|.
