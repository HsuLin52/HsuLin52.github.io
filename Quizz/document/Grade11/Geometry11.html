Geometry Grade 11 Quiz
Easy Questions
• In an equilateral triangle, what is the measure of each angle?



• Which of the following is true for the angles of a right-angled triangle?



• What is the length of the hypotenuse of a right triangle if the legs are 3 cm and 4 cm?



• In a circle, what is the term for a line segment that passes through the center and touches the circle at two points?



• What is the area of a circle with a radius of 6 cm? (Use π≈3.14)



Medium Questions
• Which of the following statements is true for an isosceles triangle?



• If the measure of an angle at the center of a circle is 80°, what is the measure of the inscribed angle subtended by the same arc?



• In a triangle, if the lengths of the sides are 7 cm, 24 cm, and 25 cm, is the triangle a right-angled triangle?



• What is the area of a sector of a circle with a radius of 10 cm and a central angle of 60°? (Use π≈3.14)



• In a triangle, if two angles measure 40° and 65°, what is the measure of the third angle?



Hard Questions
• In a right-angled triangle, the sides adjacent and opposite to the right angle measure 6 cm and 8 cm, respectively. What is the length of the hypotenuse?



• A line intersects a circle at two points. What is this line called?



• What is the angle between a tangent to a circle and the radius at the point of contact?



• A transformation that moves a figure in space without changing its shape or size is called a:



• The coordinates of a point are (2, 3). What is the image of this point after a rotation of 90° counterclockwise about the origin?
